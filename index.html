Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you visit the JhaWebDesigner portfolio website. It describes how the site works today.

1. Summary

This is a personal developer portfolio website. It does not have user accounts, a login system, a newsletter, advertising, or a contact form that stores your data. There is no server-side database behind this site collecting visitor information.

The website is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It is designed to keep working without collecting anything, and the optional visit counter described in section 4 stores only anonymous, aggregate numbers — never your identity.

2. Information this website collects

The site itself does not collect, store or sell personal information. There is no sign-up, no analytics script and no tracking pixel on this website.

Like most websites, the hosting provider that serves these pages may automatically keep standard technical server logs — for example IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time of the request. These logs are generated by the hosting infrastructure for security and reliability purposes and are not used by the site owner to identify or profile visitors. If you would like details for a specific host, contact the address in section 11 and the hosting provider's own privacy policy can be pointed to.

  • No account registration and no login.
  • No contact form that transmits or stores your message.
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics or behavioural profiling scripts.

3. Local storage and cookies

This website uses your browser's localStorage for one purpose only: to remember whether you chose the dark or light theme, so the site opens in your preferred appearance next time. The value stored is a simple word such as "dark" or "light".

  • This value stays in your own browser; it is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes it at any time.
  • The site sets no cookies at all — no advertising, no analytics and no session cookies.
  • Anonymous visit counting, when enabled, uses the database described in section 4 and does not rely on cookies.

If your browser or device is set to reject local storage, the website still works — it will simply open with the default theme each visit.

4. Anonymous visit statistics (optional Firebase counter)

This website includes an optional, off-by-default visit counter. When it is enabled by the site owner, it writes a small anonymous record to a Google Firebase Firestore database so that the total number of visits can be displayed on the page.

When enabled, the following is stored for each page view:

  • Timestamp of the visit.
  • Page path — for example /index.html.
  • Coarse device category — one of mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop or small-screen. This is derived only from screen width, not from any device identifier.
  • Referrer hostname — the site you arrived from, or the word "direct" if you typed the address or used a bookmark.
  • A random per-tab identifier — a random string generated in your browser that is discarded when you close the tab. It is not a fingerprint and cannot be used to recognise you on another site or a later visit.
  • Browser language — for example "en-IN", used only in aggregate.

What is never collected or stored: your name, email address, phone number, IP address, precise location, the content of any message you write, any file, any advertising identifier, and any cross-site tracking data.

The statistics are aggregate and are displayed only as a total visit count. If the counter is switched off by the site owner — which is its default state — this section does not apply and no records are created at all.

You can avoid the counter entirely without losing any content: enable Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control in your browser, block gstatic.com, or simply use a content blocker. The website works fully either way, and visitors who signal Do Not Track are skipped automatically.

Firebase is operated by Google. See Firebase's privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy for how that infrastructure handles data on its side.

5. Information you send by email, phone or WhatsApp

The contact buttons on this site open your own email app, dialler or WhatsApp. If you choose to get in touch, you are sharing information with those services and with the site owner:

  • Email — messages are delivered through your email provider and Google's mail service used by the site owner, and are kept as normal correspondence.
  • Phone calls and SMS — handled by your telecom operator and the site owner's mobile network.
  • WhatsApp — governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. The prefilled message is created in your browser; nothing is sent until you press send.

Information you send is used only to reply to you and to discuss the work you asked about. It is not sold, rented or shared for marketing purposes.

6. Third-party services and external links

The site itself loads no external fonts, scripts, frameworks or images — every page, style sheet, script and logo is served from this website. The only exception is the optional Firebase visit counter described in section 4: when it is switched on, the Firebase SDK is downloaded from Google's gstatic.com CDN. While the counter is off — its default state — reading these pages shares nothing with any third-party service.

Some links point to external websites (for example WhatsApp, or a project's live demo). Once you follow such a link, you are on another website and its own privacy policy applies. This site has no control over those websites.

7. Data retention

The website stores no visitor data, so there is nothing for it to retain or delete. Correspondence you send directly (email, call or WhatsApp message) is kept only as long as it is useful for answering you and for ordinary business records, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time using the contact details in section 11.

Technical server logs kept by the hosting provider are retained according to that provider's own policies.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general professional audience and is not directed at children. No personal information is knowingly collected from children, because the site collects no personal information through its own pages at all.

9. Your choices and requests

You can always:

  • Clear the saved theme preference by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Block local storage or JavaScript in your browser — the content still remains readable.
  • Ask what correspondence you have sent has been kept, and ask for it to be deleted.
  • Avoid sending any information you would rather not share.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy describes the website as it exists today. It should be reviewed and updated whenever the site adds features that change how data is handled. If any of the following are added in future, this page must be updated before those features go live:

  • Analytics or visitor-statistics tools — the optional Firebase visit counter is described in section 4; if more detailed analytics are ever added, they must be documented here first
  • A contact form that stores or forwards messages through a backend service
  • Cookies beyond the theme preference
  • Advertising, sponsorship or affiliate tracking
  • User accounts, authentication or any login
  • Embedded third-party services, widgets, fonts, maps or video players
  • Newsletters, mailing lists or push notifications

When a change is made, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised and the new activities will be described here in clear terms.
